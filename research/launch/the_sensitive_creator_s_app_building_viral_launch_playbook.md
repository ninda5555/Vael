## 2. The Zero-Cost, Zero-Overwhelm Build Roadmap
You do not need investors, a computer science degree, or money to ship a real mobile app. Follow this exact 4-step pipeline:

### Step 1: Generate the Core App (Days 1–3)
* Open **Google AI Studio (Build Mode)** (or **Lovable** / **Bolt.new**).
* Paste the **UNSENT Master Prompt** from your prompt file.
* Test it on your phone's browser. Fix only what feels clunky—do not try to add 20 extra features yet.

### Step 2: Put It Online for Free (Day 4)
* Export or sync your code to a free **GitHub** repository.
* Connect GitHub to **Vercel** or **Netlify** (100% free tier).
* Within 2 minutes, you will have a live `https://...` web app link that works smoothly on any Android or iPhone browser.

### Step 3: Turn It Into a Real Phone App (Days 5–7)
* **Fastest Option (PWA - Progressive Web App):** Add a `manifest.json` and service worker (ask AI Studio: *"Make this installable as a mobile PWA with an app icon and offline support"*). Users can tap **"Add to Home Screen"** and it opens full-screen just like a native app—with zero Play Store fees.
* **Android APK Option:** Once you have users, wrap your live URL using **PWABuilder.com** or **Capacitor** to generate an Android `.apk` / `.aab` file.

## 3. Built-In Virality: How the App Markets Itself
Sensitive people rarely brag, so your app should grow **quietly through emotional resonance**, not loud ads:

1. **The 9:16 Story Canvas:** Every exported letter or poem card is sized at `1080 × 1920` pixels—exact Instagram Story / WhatsApp Status / Reels dimensions.
2. **The "Safe Vulnerability" Toggle:** People want to share their feelings, *without* exposing private names. Having a one-tap switch that turns `"Dear Rahul,"` into `"To someone I never stopped missing,"` increases sharing by 3x.
3. **Mother-Tongue Emotional Gravity:** Typing *"আমায় কেউ বোঝে না"* (Bengali) or *"थक गया हूँ खुद को समझाते-समझाते"* (Hindi/Hinglish) hits 10x deeper than generic English quotes. Make sure your promo videos showcase Bengali, Hindi, and English letters side-by-side.
4. **The Watermark Loop:** Place a tiny, aesthetic signature at the bottom of every exported image: `written on unsent • @yourhandle`. One viral Story brings hundreds of curious clicks.

## 5. Gentle Rules for Building While Struggling in Life
When your energy is low, traditional "hustle culture" advice will only make you feel worse. Use these gentle rules instead:

* **Rule of 30 Minutes:** You do not need to work 8 hours a day. Give your app **30 quiet minutes** with a cup of tea. Small drops fill an ocean.
* **Ship at 80%:** Pisces perfectionists delay launching because they want every pixel to feel magical. Launch when it works cleanly, then improve it with real users.
* **Your Struggle Is Material, Not a Flaw:** Every lonely night, every unspoken thought, and every quiet hope you have is the exact heartbeat that will make someone else say, *"How did this app know what I was feeling?"*
